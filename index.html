Policies & Support

Clear terms. No fine-print surprises.

Listing & Verification Policy

n

Every property submitted through "Post a Property" is reviewed by our team within 24–48 hours. We verify ownership documents, land-use classification, and boundary records before a listing is marked Verified and promoted to active buyers.

Privacy Policy

n

Contact details shared while posting a property or enquiring about a listing are used solely to facilitate communication between buyers and sellers. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Refund & Cancellation

n

Listing fees, where applicable, are refundable within 7 days if a property is withdrawn before verification is complete. Post-verification withdrawals are handled on a case-by-case basis.

n

Our team assists with site visits, document checks and price negotiation guidance for every enquiry raised through the Contact section — at no extra charge to the buyer.
